## Reading it honestly

1. The MC is a fill-rate × lock × size calculation with a small failure tail bolted on. Its shape is not
   in doubt; its level depends on 22 fills. The 90-day band at 1 contract (+$22 to +$43) is the sampling
   spread of a ~+10c lock at ~3.7/day, not market risk.
2. **Size is where the money is, and print size is the binding constraint**, not capital. Median taker
   print is 4 lots, so 5 contracts fill ~5× only when the pump is big; 20 contracts ≈ 2.5× of 5. Capital
   recycles hourly ($1.90/contract-set), so even 20 contracts needs only ~$38 in flight.
3. Not modeled: wing-side impact for c > 1 (thinner wing median 409 lots, min 19 → fine at 1–5, untested
   at 20); ops/venue failure (the 09-14 stray-rest stacking class — zero loss then, but that is the real
   tail); fill-rate regime change (calm markets = fewer pumps); a bucket-hysteresis or other param change.
4. **Levers this touches are Brad's**: `V32_MAX_CONTRACTS_PER_ORDER = 2` (stops pin) and `contracts` in
   the sha-pinned params. The falsifier's promotion gate is n ≥ 60 with ≥ 10 lots depth — the MC says
   the depth clause is the one that matters.
