## 3. The three defects, named

1. **Identity.** A cancel event with `order_id None` is attributed by `None == None` to whichever pending order
   comes first (`core._apply_cancelled`). Pre-flight rejections and no-op cancels both emit that event. Nine such
   events evicted nine innocent pending orders, two of which then rested live.
2. **Ownership.** The in-flight guard (#115) starts after the pre-flight GET, so a cancel arriving during the
   pre-flight is a no-op; and an executor stand-down cancels nothing it owns while the stood-down core issues no
   actions. Orders can rest at the venue that neither side will ever cancel.
3. **Hedging truth.** `_apply_fill` ignores a rung fill whose order is not on the core's ladder. Any fill on an
   order the EXECUTOR owns -- which is the only ownership the venue recognises -- must be hedged, whatever the
   core remembers. This is the defect that cost the money, and the one that converts the other two from "messy"
   into "naked".

A fourth, upstream: the stale-wing hold flaps dozens of times an hour with no feed drop (22:00Z 39 holds, 23:00Z
36, 03:00Z 12 with 0 WS closes), and a hold that expires into a cancel-all followed by an immediate re-place is
what feeds the invariant. The invariant then stands the executor down -- correctly by its own rules -- in 3 of 12
armed windows.

## 4. What needs to happen (the re-arm gates, in build order)

Each is a reviewed PR (Opus 4.8), Brad merges. Items A-D gate the re-arm. E-H go in the same series.

**A. Hedge every owned fill (core + driver).** In `_apply_fill`, when no ladder order matches the coid, do NOT
return. Attribute the fill from the event itself (`market_ticker` -> `_sd_for_bucket_ticker`; price and count
from the event; rung/E_rung from the price vs `n_top` or labelled `orphan`), book it via `_book_rung_fill`, raise
an alarm `orphan_rung_fill_hedged`, and let the coalesced wing batch fire -- INCLUDING when `st.stood_down`
(a stood-down core must still hedge; it must not quote). Golden test: replay the 02:00Z event order and assert a
`TAKE_WINGS` for 2 lots on the two strikes.

**B. Cancel identity (events + core).** Give `OrderCancelled` an optional `client_order_id` (default None;
V3.2-compatible). The executor's pre-flight rejection and every no-op cancel carry the coid. `_apply_cancelled`
attributes by order_id when present, else by client_order_id, and NEVER matches `None == None`. A rejected place
drops exactly the rejected pending order. Test: nine rejections against eleven pending orders leave the two
accepted ones on the ladder.

**C. Ownership (executor).** (i) `_inflight_creates.add(coid)` at the TOP of `_place_rest_async` (and of the
batch path's per-chunk create), before the pacer and the pre-flight; the deferral covers the whole call. (ii) On
an executor stand-down (`rest_invariant_violation` or any `stand_down_reason`), immediately cancel every LIVE
record in `rest_book` on the cancel lane, confirm each, and emit the `OrderCancelled(oid, coid, filled)` events so
any racing fill is booked and hedged by A. (iii) The stood-down executor keeps answering `TAKE_WINGS` /
`RETRY_WING` / cancel actions (it refuses only new rests and amends). Test: stand-down with two live rests ->
two DELETEs, zero rests left; a fill that lands between the stand-down and the DELETE -> wings taken.

**D. No re-place inside the cancel-confirm window (core).** The stale-wing resume path waits like the
bucket-change path: `_place_all` only when `outstanding_cancels == 0` and the ladder is clear. Test: hold ->
cancel-all -> resume 200 ms later -> no `PLACE_REST` until the eleventh `OrderCancelled`; then eleven places.

**E. Accounting from executor truth.** The window summary's `lots_filled`, `rung_fills`, `one_legged`,
`held_legs` and the S1_LEGGED day-guard record reconcile against the executor's fills (`rest_fill` /
`self.fills`) and the venue's `/portfolio/fills` at window end; any executor-known fill the core did not book is
surfaced as `unbooked_fill` and counted one-legged unless hedged. The ledger `alarms` field includes executor
alarms. Test: the 02:00Z row rebuilt from the journal reads lots 2, one_legged True, alarms 10.

**F. Hold sensitivity.** Measure `stand_down_hold` counts per window since 09-30 against feed gaps; a hold
that fires with `feed_gap_max_s < 0.4` and no WS close is a clock artefact. Either raise `freshness_max_age_s`
for the strike books, or require the hold to persist N ticks before it cancels. Decide from the measurement, not
by feel; the number goes in the Registration.

**G. #115 residuals.** The batch create path (`_place_one_chunk_async`) gets the same in-flight / deferred-cancel
handling as the single path (covered by C(i)); a fill in the ack->DELETE gap of a deferred-cancelled slot is
booked and hedged (covered by A).

**H. Supervisor dedupe.** The 2026-10-02 21:00Z close was skipped (`close_already_run`) because a ledger row for
that close already existed before the window. Find the writer of that row (daily replay / backfill) and key the
dedupe on a live-run marker, not on row presence.

## 5. What the belts did right

The venue pre-flight invariant caught 17 > 11 at 15:00Z and 9 duplicates at 02:00Z, both before any orphan
filled on the first night and before more than two filled on the second. The websocket fill handler saw every
fill and journaled it, which is why this write-up exists. The frozen one-legged pin (<= 2) is doing exactly what
it was written for on 09-30: the next naked contract ends Test Fire #2.
